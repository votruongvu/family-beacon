# Project Charter

## Product Purpose

Family Beacon exists so family members can know where their loved ones are and can ask for or offer help quickly, without any of it happening behind someone's back. Families caring for an elderly parent or a young child need a simple, trustworthy answer to "are they safe, and where were they last?" — and a fast way to raise the alarm when they are not.

The product provides practical everyday reassurance, not surveillance. Every product decision is judged by whether it helps a family member locate a loved one, helps someone request or answer a call for help, preserves the located person's awareness and control, and works reliably enough for ordinary family use.

## Target Users

- Adults caring for an elderly parent.
- Parents and guardians caring for a young child.
- Family members who want simple, mutual location sharing among themselves.

Every one of them is also a potential help-seeker: any member may be the person who needs assistance. When trade-offs arise, the needs of the person being located — their awareness and their control — take priority over the convenience of the person doing the locating.

## Desired Outcomes

- Families gain everyday reassurance about whether a loved one is safe and where they were last known to be.
- A family member in trouble can raise an alarm within seconds and see that someone has taken it up.
- Relatives who want to help can reach that person quickly, in person or by phone.
- Every located person stays continuously aware of, and in control of, what they share.
- Families can trust what the product tells them, because it is honest about how current a location is and about what it cannot guarantee.
- The least technical member of the family — including an elderly relative — can use it unaided.

## Product Capabilities

- **Simple identity** — family members identify themselves without passwords or account-management overhead.
- **Trusted family membership** — forming a family group whose members join only by explicit invitation and acceptance.
- **Consent-based location sharing** — each person controls whether, and when, their own location is shared with their family.
- **Shared family awareness** — seeing where family members are, together with how current and how precise that information is.
- **Assistance requests** — a deliberate way for a member to signal that they need help now, with their location attached.
- **Assistance response** — alerting the family, confirming who is responding, and reaching or getting to the person who asked.

## Boundaries & Principles

**Permanent principles — these define the product and are not traded away:**

- No hidden tracking. There is no covert mode, no secret member, and no way to conceal from a person that they are being located.
- Sharing is self-controlled. No one — not even the family owner — can turn on another person's sharing for them, and any person can pause or stop sharing at any time.
- Membership is consented. Nobody becomes part of a family without accepting for themselves, and family data stays inside that family.
- Honesty over reassurance. The product never presents an old location as a current one, and never implies help is on the way when all it did was send a notification.
- Family Beacon is not an emergency service. It depends on connectivity, permissions, and a relative being available, and never claims to replace local emergency services.
- Data minimalism. The product keeps what is needed to answer "where are they now" — not a record of where people have been.
- Accessible to the whole family. Elderly members are first-class users, not an afterthought.

**Current boundaries — outside the product unless deliberately reintroduced through a reviewed change:**

- Movement history, playback, geofences, and arrival or departure alerts.
- Prediction of where someone will be.
- Messaging, calling, or social features beyond reaching someone during a request for help.
- Belonging to more than one family at a time.
- Integration with emergency dispatch services.
- Remote access to another person's device, such as microphone or camera.
